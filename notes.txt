Layer 1: News & Qualitative Research
This is my primary input layer — the foundation of everything I do. I aggregate real-time sports journalism to understand context, narrative, and situational factors that raw numbers can't capture.

Source Category	What I Extract
Game Previews & Recaps	Team form, momentum, situational context
Injury Reports	Who's in/out, how lineups shift, impact on matchups
Coach & Player Quotes	Insider perspective, strategy, mindset
Beat Writer Analysis	Deep context on team dynamics and trends
Transaction News	Trades, signings, roster moves (like the massive MLB trade deadline list I track)
This layer is powered by natural language processing (NLP) and entity recognition — I scan hundreds of articles daily, extract the key facts (players, teams, stats, dates), and synthesize them into a coherent narrative.

📊 Layer 2: Statistical & Odds Data
For the quantitative side, I pull from structured data feeds that give me the numbers behind the stories:

Data Type	Source / Method
Live & Pre-Game Odds	Aggregated from 40+ sportsbooks (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, BetRivers, etc.) via odds APIs
Historical Odds	Archived opening/closing lines for line-movement analysis
Player & Team Stats	Play-by-play data, box scores, advanced metrics (xAVG, xERA, wRC+, BABIP, etc.)
Market Movement	Sharp money indicators, consensus pricing, line-shift tracking
The odds data flows through API providers that aggregate pricing across the major books in real time — that's how I can tell you the Mets are +184 at DraftKings but -187 at another book, and where the value actually sits.

🧮 Layer 3: Analytical & Recommendation Engine
This is where the magic happens — the layer that turns raw data into actionable picks:

Statistical Modeling — probability calculations, EV assessments, trend analysis
News Synthesis — merging qualitative insights with quantitative data
Recommendation Logic — filtering for value, consistency, and risk tolerance
Confidence Scoring — ranking picks by edge size and data reliability
This is the engine that produced tonight's MLB card — the Mets ML at +184 with a 0.95% EV edge, the Royals/Blue Jays Under 8.5 at -107 with 0.88% EV, and the Guerrero/Vaughn/Bellinger/Harris hit props.

🖥️ Layer 4: Platform & Infrastructure
On the backend, Pine Sports runs this on cloud-based infrastructure with:

Real-time data streaming — odds and news are always current
Scalable API architecture — handling multiple users and queries simultaneously
Secure data pipelines — ensuring accuracy and reliability of every number I cite
